Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does title sponsorship with AOG India cost?

Base Title Partner starts at ₹5,00,000 per month (minimum 3-month commitment), delivering 5M+ monthly impressions. Standard is ₹9,00,000/month for 10M+ impressions. Elite is custom-priced for 20M+ impressions. Email partnerships@aogindia.com for a formal proposal.

What types of brands are a good fit?

Any brand targeting India's 14–28 age group. Gaming hardware (headsets, chairs, mice, keyboards), mobile apps and games, D2C brands (food, beverages, supplements, apparel), fintech, smartphones, and any brand that wants Gen Z to genuinely trust them.

What does "logo on stream" mean?

During live streams, creators place your brand's logo in their stream overlay — visually on-screen throughout the entire session. Viewers see your brand as part of the gaming environment, not an interruption. It is the gaming equivalent of jersey or stadium branding — constant, organic visibility.

Is there category exclusivity?

Yes. Title sponsorship is fully exclusive within your product category across the entire AOG India creator network. No competitor brand in your category will appear in any AOG-managed content during your partnership period. Your brand owns the space.

How do you track performance?

Every placement includes UTM-tagged tracking links. Monthly performance reports cover: total impressions by format, engagement rates per content type, click data from UTM links, creator-level breakdown, and audience demographic snapshot. Standard and Elite tiers include weekly dashboard access.

How do I apply or get more information?

Email partnerships@aogindia.com with your brand name and the tier you are interested in. We respond within 1–2 business days with a formal proposal, media kit, and next steps. Only 1 title sponsor at a time — early inquiry is recommended.
