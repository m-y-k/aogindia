Brand FAQs

Common questions from brand managers and marketing teams exploring gaming influencer campaigns with AOG India Esports.

How much does a gaming influencer campaign cost in India?

Costs vary by creator tier, content format, and campaign scope. Product seeding with micro-creators starts from ₹50,000. Mid-tier BGMI or YouTube gaming campaigns range ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000. Full multi-creator campaigns can exceed ₹10,00,000. AOG India Esports provides custom proposals — contact us for a tailored quote.

What types of brands can run gaming influencer campaigns with AOG?

We work with gaming hardware brands, mobile game publishers, consumer electronics, smartphones, D2C products, beverages, and lifestyle brands targeting India's gaming and Gen Z (18–30) audience. Non-gaming brands can also reach gaming audiences through creator integrations.

How do I run a BGMI creator campaign for my brand?

We handle it end-to-end. AOG shortlists relevant BGMI and mobile gaming creators, briefs them on your brand, manages sponsored gameplay content and deliverables, and provides post-campaign performance reporting. Contact us to start.

Can gaming influencer marketing work for non-gaming brands?

Yes. India's gaming and Gen Z creator audience is highly receptive to tech, consumer electronics, D2C products, beverages, fashion, and lifestyle brands. Our creators integrate your product authentically — reaching millions of engaged 18–30 year-old viewers.

How quickly can you launch a campaign?

After brief alignment and creator shortlisting, most campaigns launch within 7–14 business days. Time-sensitive launches (game releases, product drops) can be fast-tracked. Email us directly for urgent timelines.
