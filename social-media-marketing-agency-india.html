How to Market to Gen Z Consumers in India

Gen Z marketing in India requires a fundamentally different approach than traditional media. Here's what works.

Skip the Ads, Use Creators

84% of Gen Z in India skip or ignore traditional digital ads. Creator-native content embedded within gaming and tech YouTube videos or Instagram Reels is consumed as regular content, not advertising.

Mobile-First, Short-Form

Gen Z in India primarily consumes content on mobile. Short-form video on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts dominates attention. Brand campaigns built for short-form video outperform long-form by 3× in Gen Z recall.

Community Over Broadcasting

Gaming communities on Discord, YouTube, and Instagram are tight-knit. Creator campaigns that feel community-native — not broadcast advertising — generate authentic engagement and word-of-mouth.

Authenticity Drives Conversion

Gen Z can detect inauthentic brand messaging instantly. Creator partnerships that allow genuine voice — honest reviews, real product opinions — consistently outperform scripted brand campaigns in conversion metrics.
