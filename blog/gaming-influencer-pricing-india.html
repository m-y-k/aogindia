Gaming Influencer Campaign Pricing in India 2026: What Brands Actually Pay

6 min read

"How much does a gaming influencer campaign cost in India?" — it's the first question every brand manager asks, and it's one of the hardest to answer without real market data. There is no fixed rate card. Pricing varies by creator size, game niche, content format, usage rights, and exclusivity.

This guide breaks down real pricing benchmarks for gaming creator campaigns in India as of 2026 — based on what we see working at AOG India Esports. No inflated agencies quotes, no guesswork. Just transparent data to help brands plan smarter.

Important: These are typical market rates, not fixed prices. Actual costs depend on creator demand, exclusivity requirements, content rights, and campaign scope. Use these ranges as a planning benchmark, then get custom quotes for your specific campaign.

Pricing by Content Format

Paid Integration (60–90 sec in a regular video)

The most common format — brand gets 60 to 90 seconds within a regular gaming video. Generally priced at 60–80% of the dedicated video rate. Best balance of reach and cost-efficiency.

Dedicated Sponsored Video (full video)

Entire video is about the brand — review, unboxing, challenge, or setup video. Commands a 20–40% premium over standard integrations. Delivers maximum messaging time but should only be used when the product genuinely interests the creator's audience.

YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels

Short-form content (15–60 seconds). Typically priced at 30–50% of a long-form integration fee. Excellent for app installs, sale announcements, and awareness campaigns. High viral potential, especially for visually strong products.

What Factors Push Prices Higher?

  • Exclusivity. If you require the creator not to work with competitors for 30–90 days, expect a 25–50% premium.
  • Extended usage rights. Using creator content in your own paid ads requires additional licensing fees — typically ₹5,000–₹50,000 depending on the duration and platform.
  • Turnaround time. Rush campaigns (delivery under 7 days) attract a 15–30% urgency premium.
  • Tier 1 creators in peak season. Festival seasons (Diwali, IPL, New Year) see creator fees spike 30–60% as more brands compete.
  • Product type. Creators in the tech niche (gaming hardware, smartphones) charge more than gaming content creators, because their audience is more affluent and conversion rates are higher.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gaming Influencer Pricing

Can I negotiate gaming creator rates?

Yes, almost always. Most Indian gaming creators have flexible rates, especially for brands they genuinely like or long-term partnerships. Multi-video packages, exclusive agreements, or product-only deals (for smaller creators) can significantly reduce per-video cost.

Are gaming influencer campaigns cheaper than Google or Meta ads for the same reach?

For brand awareness, gaming creator campaigns often deliver better CPM (cost per thousand impressions) than Meta ads among the 16–26 gaming demographic. For direct conversion, Meta retargeting may outperform. The ideal strategy combines both: creator campaigns for awareness and trust-building, performance ads for retargeting.

What is the minimum budget for a gaming creator campaign in India?

Product seeding campaigns can start for the cost of the product (plus shipping). Cash campaigns can start from ₹30,000–₹50,000 for 3–5 micro-creators. We don't recommend going below this because multi-creator campaigns significantly outperform single-creator campaigns in organic reach and audience trust.
