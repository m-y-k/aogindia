How to Run a BGMI Creator Campaign in India: A Complete Brand Guide (2026)

By Mohammad Yusuf Khan, Founder — AOG India Esports
22 September 2026
8 min read

BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India) has over 100 million registered users in India — making it the most played mobile battle royale in the country. For brands targeting the 16–28 age demographic, BGMI creator campaigns offer unparalleled access to a hyper-engaged, purchase-ready audience that traditional advertising channels simply cannot reach.

This guide explains exactly how BGMI influencer campaigns work, how to choose the right creators, what content formats deliver results, and what you should realistically budget — based on how we run campaigns at AOG India Esports.

Who is this guide for? Brand managers, digital marketing leads, media buyers, and agency teams planning to run BGMI creator campaigns in India for the first time — or looking to improve existing campaigns.

What Is a BGMI Creator Campaign?

A BGMI creator campaign is a paid collaboration between a brand and one or more BGMI YouTubers or Instagram creators. The creator integrates your brand — product, app, service, or message — into their gaming content in exchange for a fee or free product. The content typically reaches their existing subscriber or follower base, who are passionate BGMI players.

Unlike display advertising, BGMI creator campaigns benefit from creator trust. When a creator their audience follows for years endorses a product, the recommendation carries significantly more weight than a banner ad.

Content Formats for BGMI Campaigns

1. Paid Promotion Integration (Long-Form YouTube)

The creator dedicates 60–90 seconds of a standard BGMI gameplay video to your brand. This is the most common format. It feels organic because it's embedded in content the viewer already chose to watch. The creator typically shows the product on-screen, explains its benefits in their own words, and shares a discount code or link.

2. Dedicated Sponsored Video

The entire video is built around your product — an unboxing, a review, or a challenge using your product. Delivers maximum brand exposure but costs more and requires the creator to have genuine enthusiasm for your product.

3. YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels

30–60 second short-form content. Excellent for product seeding, app installs, and quick-reach campaigns. Lower cost than long-form but less time to explain product features.

4. Live Stream Integration

Brand shoutout or product display during a BGMI live stream. Reaches the creator's most loyal fans — viewers who have specifically chosen to watch a livestream are highly engaged. Includes verbal endorsement, on-screen overlay, and sometimes a pinned link in chat.

Step-by-Step: How to Run a BGMI Creator Campaign

  1. Define your campaign objective. Brand awareness (maximum reach), product consideration (creator explains features), or conversion/install (tracked link with discount code). Your objective determines which creator tier and content format to prioritise.
  2. Set your budget. See the pricing breakdown below. Account for creator fees, product/sample cost (if seeding), UTM tracking setup, and reporting.
  3. Creator shortlisting. Filter by game focus (BGMI, BGMI + Free Fire, or all-mobile), subscriber tier, average view count, engagement rate, and audience demographics. Avoid creators with suspicious sub spikes or very low like-to-view ratios.
  4. Brief creation. Write a clear brand brief: key messages, mandatory inclusions (brand name, product name, CTA, discount code), restricted content (competitors, controversial topics), and video/post deadlines.
  5. Content review. Review creator content before it goes live. Most professional creators expect one round of revisions.
  6. Go live & track. Track performance via UTM-tagged links, promo code redemptions, and platform analytics. BGMI creator content typically peaks in views within the first 72 hours of upload.
  7. Post-campaign report. Compile total reach, views, click-through rate, and cost-per-click (CPC). Use this to benchmark for future campaigns.

Common Mistakes Brands Make in BGMI Campaigns

  • Choosing creators by subscriber count alone. A 500K creator with 2% engagement outperforms a 2M creator with 0.3% engagement in almost every measurable metric.
  • Over-scripting the creator. BGMI audiences are sharp. If the promotion sounds like an ad read, they skip. Give creators a brief with key points, then let them speak naturally.
  • No tracking in place. Without UTM links or unique promo codes, you cannot attribute results to the campaign. Always set up tracking before going live.
  • Single-creator campaigns with unrealistic expectations. One video rarely goes viral. Multi-creator campaigns with 5–10 creators generate compounding reach and search presence.
  • Ignoring Instagram Reels. Many BGMI creators have active Instagram audiences too. Running the same campaign across both YouTube and Instagram extends reach at marginal additional cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to launch a BGMI creator campaign?

From brief alignment and creator shortlisting to first content going live — typically 7 to 14 business days. Time-sensitive product launches can be fast-tracked.

Can I run a BGMI campaign with a budget under ₹1,00,000?

Yes. A micro-creator product seeding campaign (3–5 BGMI creators, 30K–100K subscribers each) with a combined fee budget of ₹50,000–₹80,000 is a realistic and effective starting point for most brands.

Do BGMI creator campaigns work for non-gaming brands?

Absolutely. BGMI audiences in India are receptive to tech accessories, energy drinks, D2C products, fintech apps, and lifestyle brands — especially when the promotion is integrated naturally into gameplay content.
