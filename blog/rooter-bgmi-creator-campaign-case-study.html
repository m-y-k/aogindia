When Rooter — India's leading gaming and esports streaming platform — needed to deepen penetration into the BGMI community, they partnered with AOG India Esports for a precision gaming creator campaign. The brief: reach engaged BGMI players on Instagram with native, shareable content that felt organic — not corporate.

What followed was one of the most share-heavy short-form gaming campaigns executed in India, with a virality index dominated by peer-to-peer WhatsApp shares across gamer communities — the hardest metric to fake and the most valuable signal of genuine audience resonance.

The Core Insight: Most gaming marketing agencies optimize for views. AOG India Esports optimized for shares — the real signal of content gamers genuinely want to pass to their friends. Result: 56.5% of all engagement came from shares, not likes.

Campaign Overview

Campaign Details

  • Client: Rooter (Gaming & Esports Streaming Platform)
  • Creator: FizZer BGMI
  • Creator Reach: 112K YouTube · 55K Instagram
  • Format: 2 x Instagram Reels
  • Target: Gen-Z BGMI players, India (16–25 yrs)
  • Distribution: Instagram + WhatsApp gamer communities

Campaign Results

  • Reel 1 Views: >1.4 Million
  • Total Likes: 44,700+ (42.1%)
  • Total Shares: 58,600+ (56.5%)
  • Reel 2 Shares: 51,900+ shares alone
  • Comments: 1,340+ (1.3%)
  • Reported Sales: ~₹1 Lakh

Why FizZer BGMI? The Creator Selection Rationale

AOG India Esports selected FizZer BGMI based on data-driven audience fit analysis — not just follower count. Here is why FizZer was the optimal creator for Rooter's objectives:

  • Audience Demographic Match: FizZer's core audience is Gen-Z Indian mobile gamers aged 16–25 — Rooter's exact primary user acquisition target.
  • Engagement Quality Over Quantity: With 112K YouTube subscribers, FizZer is a micro-creator. Micro-creators in gaming consistently outperform mega-creators in engagement depth, comment quality, and share velocity.
  • Native BGMI Authority: FizZer's content positions them as a trusted peer — not a distant celebrity. Rooter's recommendation from FizZer carries the weight of a friend's endorsement.
  • Cross-Platform Presence: Dual YouTube + Instagram presence allowed Reels virality to drive YouTube audience crossover — maximizing total impression surface.

  • Micro-creators outperform mega-creators in gaming: FizZer's 112K-subscriber audience delivered 1.4M views because the audience is hyper-engaged, not just large.
