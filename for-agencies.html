Agency FAQs

Common questions from agency teams exploring a creator marketing partnership with AOG India Esports.

Can we use AOG India Esports as a white-label partner?

Yes. We can operate fully behind the scenes — without AOG India Esports branding visible to your client. All creator communication and deliverables are handled under your agency's workflow, keeping your client relationship intact.

What types of agencies do you work with?

We work with creative agencies, digital marketing agencies, media agencies, PR agencies, social media agencies, influencer marketing agencies, performance marketing agencies, content agencies, event agencies, and brand strategy agencies — managing campaigns for clients across India.

How quickly can you turn around a creator shortlist?

For most campaigns, we deliver an initial creator shortlist with audience data and rate information within 48–72 hours of receiving a complete brief. Complex or niche campaigns may take slightly longer.

Do you only work with gaming creators?

No. While gaming (especially BGMI) is our core specialization, our creator network also includes tech reviewers, gadget creators, and Gen Z lifestyle influencers. If you need creators for a gaming, tech, or youth-focused campaign, we can help.

Can you handle product seeding for our client's campaign?

Yes. We coordinate product seeding campaigns — managing product delivery to creators, creator briefing, content review, and publishing timelines. This works well for gaming accessories, tech gadgets, mobile devices, and consumer products.

Is there a minimum campaign size to work with AOG India Esports?

We work with agencies on both single-campaign projects and ongoing retainer arrangements. There is no fixed minimum — reach out with your brief and we'll discuss what's feasible and what makes sense for your client's budget.
