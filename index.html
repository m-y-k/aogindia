Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from brands and agency teams exploring gaming, tech, and Gen Z creator campaigns with AOG India Esports.

What is AOG India Esports?

AOG India Esports (Alliance Of Gamers) is a gaming, tech, and Gen Z influencer marketing agency based in India. We connect brands and marketing agencies with 200+ gaming, tech, and youth creators for influencer campaigns, product seeding, and creator-led brand activations on YouTube and Instagram.

What types of brands and clients can work with AOG India Esports?

We work directly with gaming hardware brands, gaming accessories, mobile gaming titles, tech companies, consumer electronics brands, and D2C brands targeting India's gaming and Gen Z audience. We also partner with creative, digital, media, PR, social, and influencer agencies that need gaming or tech creator access for their clients.

Which platforms do AOG creators work on?

Our creators are primarily active on YouTube and Instagram. Content formats include long-form gameplay videos, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and live streams — giving brands multiple touchpoints to authentically reach India's gaming audience.

How does creator selection work?

We match brands with creators based on audience demographics, content niche, engagement quality, subscriber reach, and campaign objectives. Every creator in the AOG network is curated — selected for audience quality and brand suitability, not just follower count.

What types of campaigns can brands run with AOG?

Brands can run dedicated influencer campaigns, sponsored gameplay integrations, product reviews, unboxing content, long-term creator sponsorships, and co-created marketing campaigns. AOG handles campaign strategy, creator briefing, content execution, and performance reporting end-to-end.

How do I get started with AOG India Esports?

Fill in the contact form below, or email us at partnerships@aogindia.com. Brands: tell us your campaign goals and target audience. Agencies: tell us about your client and creator requirements. We'll respond with a tailored proposal or partnership brief within 1–2 business days.

How much does a gaming influencer campaign cost in India?

Gaming influencer campaign costs in India vary by creator tier, content format, and scope. Product seeding campaigns with micro-creators typically start from ₹50,000–₹1,00,000. Mid-tier BGMI or YouTube gaming creator campaigns range from ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000. Full-scale multi-creator campaigns can exceed ₹10,00,000. AOG India Esports provides custom proposals — contact us for a tailored quote based on your goals and budget.

How do I market a mobile game in India using creators?

Effective mobile game marketing in India starts with the right creators. AOG India Esports selects BGMI, mobile gaming, and Gen Z creators matched to your game's genre and target audience. We brief creators on gameplay highlights and key messages, run dedicated gameplay videos, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels, and track performance via views, engagement, and install metrics — all managed end-to-end.

Finding the right gaming influencer in India means matching audience demographics, niche, and engagement quality to your campaign — not just follower count.
