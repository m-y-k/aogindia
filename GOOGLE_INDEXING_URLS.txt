**Why logo appears on tab but not Google Search:**

- **Browser Tab**: Shows `favicon-64.svg` ✅ (correctly configured)
- **Google Search Results**: Uses JSON-LD `logo` property + `og:image`

**Current Setup:**
- JSON-LD `logo`: `https://aogindia.com/images/aog-logo.webp` (256x256) - Used by Google for search result logos
- `og:image`: `https://aogindia.com/images/aog-og.jpg` (1200x630) - Used for social sharing (Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp)

**To fix logo appearing on Google Search:**

1. Ensure Google has re-crawled your pages:
   - In Google Search Console, click "Request Indexing" on your homepage
   - Submit the sitemap at https://aogindia.com/sitemap.xml

2. Verify structured data is valid:
   - Use Google's Rich Results Test: https://search.google.com/test/rich-results
   - Test URL: https://aogindia.com/

3. Check that images are accessible:
   - Visit: https://aogindia.com/images/aog-logo.webp
   - Visit: https://aogindia.com/images/aog-og.jpg

4. Recommended: Add explicit logo `og:image` for Google:
   Consider adding this meta tag alongside existing og:image:
   <meta property="og:image" content="https://aogindia.com/images/aog-logo.webp">
   
   Or ensure your aog-og.jpg contains your logo prominently at the left/right side.
